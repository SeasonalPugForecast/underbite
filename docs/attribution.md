# Attribution and licences

Every source of data in Underbite, every bundled dataset and every map layer is credited in the app
(Settings, About, Attribution) and in the Sources card of each spot. This page lists what each one
asks for and where its terms are stated. The in-app text is the same: it comes from one table in the
core, and a test checks that every source the providers can name has an entry.

## Not for navigation

Underbite is not for navigation. Tides, currents, depths, charts and every other value it shows are
for planning a day on the water only. They can be wrong, late or missing. Never rely on them to steer
a vessel or to judge a hazard, and always follow official charts, notices and local regulations.
Charts, bathymetry, depth contours, current layers and storm layers also carry "Not for navigation."
on the map. The GEBCO Grid "should NOT be used for navigation or for any other purpose involving
safety at sea".

## Regulations

Fishing regulations change during the year. Underbite does not state limits, seasons or licence rules;
it links to each agency's own page, with the date the link was checked. Regulations are links, never
data. Access areas are shown as their source records them, not as permission to fish, launch or park.

## Credits that are read when the data are, not kept

- **GEBCO:** the grid's release year, the acknowledgement and its DOI are read from the GEBCO service's
  capabilities each time the layer is drawn. The page in the app says where they are read from.
- **Copernicus Marine:** "Generated using E.U. Copernicus Marine Service Information" and the DOI of
  each product a layer draws, taken from the layer catalogue.
- **NOAA National Water Prediction Service:** the `dataAttribution` lines in each forecast response are
  shown with the forecast.
- **Weather providers:** the credit line each provider's library asks for.
