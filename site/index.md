# Underbite

A free iPhone app for anglers, salt water and fresh: tides and currents, river flow, water
temperature and clarity, weather, waves, sun and moon, charts and depth, access, stocking and
seasons, for a spot or for a trip. It has no account, no ads and no tracking. Every source, card,
unit, limit and map layer can be set the way you fish.

- [Privacy policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/privacy/)
- [Support]({{ site.baseurl }}/support/)
- [Source code and issues](https://github.com/SeasonalPugForecast/underbite)

The code is MIT licensed. The data comes from the public services listed in the app and in the
privacy policy, under their own terms, and every screen credits its source. Charts and current
layers are not for navigation.
